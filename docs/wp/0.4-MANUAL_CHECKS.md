# WP-0.4 — manual checks (owner)

One check decides acceptance criterion 2 of [12 §7](../specs/12-roadmap.md): **a ±12 % stretch of
the drum fixture must show no audible flamming.** Everything else in WP-0.4 is automated.

Status: **not yet run.** ADR-0002 stays `Proposed` until you have listened.

## Produce the renders

```sh
cargo run --release --manifest-path crates/bragi-dsp/stretch-lab/Cargo.toml -p stretch-lab --bin render_listen
```

Output goes to `crates/bragi-dsp/stretch-lab/renders/` (gitignored — never committed). It writes,
for the drum loop and the 440 Hz tone, 32-bit float WAVs at 48 kHz:

- `<name>_original.wav` — the source, for A/B.
- `<name>_88_{preview,render}.wav` — stretched to 88 % length.
- `<name>_112_{preview,render}.wav` — stretched to 112 % length (the acceptance ratio).
- `<name>_pitch{+7,-5}.wav` — pitch shifted at ratio 1.0.

`preview` is the real-time tier (`Quality::Preview`, 1024-point FFT); `render` is the offline
tier (`Quality::Render`, 2048-point FFT).

## What to listen for

Load the WAVs in any audio editor and A/B each pair. Playback the original first, then the
stretched version, aligned so the **first kick** lines up (the stretched version is delayed by the
algorithmic latency: 1024 frames / 21 ms for `preview`, 2048 frames / 43 ms for `render`).

1. **No flamming (the acceptance criterion).** On `<name>_112_*` and `<name>_88_*`, each kick and
   snare must still sound like **one** hit. Listen especially for:
   - a **double attack** ("da-drum") at a hit — the artefact this check is about;
   - a **soft ghost hit** a few tens of milliseconds *before* a real hit (pre-echo);
   - a **buzzy / flanged** attack instead of a clean one.

   Test with and without headphones; headphones make a small pre-echo easier to hear.

2. **Solid, unmoving beat.** The stretched loop should stay rhythmically even — no hit should
   audibly slide early or late relative to the others.

3. **No audible "phasing" / warble** on the decaying tails (compare against the original).

4. **Pitch shift is clean and the length is right.** On `<name>_pitch+7` / `_pitch-5`, the drum
   loop should sound like the same loop transposed, still 4.0 s long (not faster/slower), with no
   obvious metallic ring. The 440 Hz tone should move to ~659 Hz / ~330 Hz without tremolo.

5. **Stereo image.** The drums should stay centred; a hit must not jump to one side.

## What "pass" means

- Kick and snare: **no double attack at ±12 %** on either quality tier.
- You may hear slight smearing of the hi-frequency content (the vocoder's known limitation) — that
  is acceptable and is recorded in ADR-0002. A clear **flam** is not.

If it fails on the `render` tier only, note that in ADR-0002: the fallback is to lower
`Quality::Render::fft_size` or to adopt Signalsmith Stretch for offline renders (see ADR-0002
"Decision"), whose feasibility is already demonstrated.

## Record the result

Add a **Listening notes** line to ADR-0002 (and flip its `Status` to `Accepted`) with the date,
which files you listened to, and the verdict. That is the only thing standing between WP-0.4 and
"Done".

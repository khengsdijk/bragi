# WP-2.4 — Manual checks

Listening checks for the instant paint brushes. Run on the owner's speakers or headphones with
`pnpm dev:backend` + `pnpm dev:web`, on a real song (upload, wait for the stems).

1. **Space while playing.** Press Play, press `B` (Paint), pick Space and paint slowly across the
   drums for a few bars, then quickly. Listen for: no clicks, dropouts or cut reverb tails while
   the stroke grows (the preview keeps the same reverb instance); on release the reverb does not
   restart; the edges of the region fade in and out over ~50 ms, not with a step.
2. **Pen pressure** (a tablet or Apple Pencil via Sidecar): press lightly, then harder. The stroke
   on the canvas gets more opaque and the effect deeper. The scroll wheel while painting does the
   same with a mouse.
3. **Every brush once.** Warmth, Brighter, Darker, Lo-fi, Louder, Quieter, Filter sweep (rising and
   falling) on a stem while playing: each is audible, goes the way its name says, and is limited
   to the painted bars and stems.
4. **Undo while playing.** After a Space stroke, press Cmd+Z: the reverb tail fades naturally.
5. **Remove.** Paint Remove over two bars of the vocals: the gap fades in and out (no click);
   Cmd+Z brings the vocals back.
6. **Keyboard.** Focus a part in the grid, open the section menu (Shift+F10) → Apply brush…,
   choose Warmth on two instruments, Apply: one history entry with a plain label.

Record results (pass/fail, notes, machine and output device) under each item.

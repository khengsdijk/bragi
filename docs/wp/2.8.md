# WP-2.8 — Inpaint, erase & fill, AI transitions, planner generation steps

- **Spec refs:** [09 §4.3–4.4, §6](../../specs/09-generation.md), [06 §3, §5](../../specs/06-ai-planner.md), [07 §4.1, §4.8, §6](../../specs/07-song-canvas.md), [02 §3.5, §4](../../specs/02-song-model.md), [03 §5.5](../../specs/03-audio-engine.md)
- **Depends on:** 2.6, 2.7 (Done)
- **Size:** M (in practice L: three capabilities' worth of UI)
- **Branch:** `wp/2.8-generation`

## Goal

Generation inside the song, not only at its edges: **Fill gap** after an erase (`generate.inpaint`),
**Smooth with AI** on a section boundary after a move (`generate.transition`), and planner plans
whose variants include `generate` steps, so a lasso + prompt can come back with a generative idea
next to the ops-only ones.

**Acceptance (12):** M2 demo 4 (lasso + prompt with a generative variant) end to end. Fill-gap
candidates pass the gates on 4 of 5 fixtures.

## Contracts (first commit, Opus)

`packages/schemas/jobs/capabilities/`: `generate.inpaint.schema.json` (`GenerateInpaintParams`:
`context`, `gapBars`, `overlapBars` (default 1), `prompt?`, `seed?`, `tier`, `n`; input assets
`before` and `after`), `generate.transition.schema.json` (`GenerateTransitionParams`: `context`,
`lengthBars` 1–4, `prompt?`, `seed?`, `tier`, `n`; input assets `before` (end of A, ≤ 8 bars) and
`after` (start of B, ≤ 8 bars)), and one result blob for both, `generate.fill-result.schema.json`
(`GenerateFillResult`: like the extend result, spliced at both ends: `startOffsetSamples` relative to
the gap start, `endOffsetSamples` relative to the gap end, `seamScore` + `endSeamScore`). Job outputs
stream exactly like extend: `assets.candidate_<i>` + `blobs.candidate_<i>` per `partial`.

## Approach

### A. Worker: inpaint, transition, two-sided gates, fill bench (Python)

- `capabilities/inpaint.py` and `capabilities/transition.py` on the A2 machinery (routing, partial
  streaming, regeneration rounds, metrics). ACE-Step: native repaint of the middle span of
  `before + silence(gap) + after` (09 §4.3: native infill); transition = the same with
  `lengthBars` centred on the boundary. Fake backend: a deterministic fill from the contexts (loop
  of `before`'s last bars crossfaded into `after`), so the e2e passes the gates on any song.
- Two-sided gates: run 09 §6 at the start seam against `before` and, mirrored, at the end seam
  against `after` (B already mirrors for backward); loudness match uses `after` for the tail
  (WP-2.7 fix). Result blob per the contract.
- Register both in the generate worker, `capabilities.yaml`, `config/generation_routes.yaml`.
- Bench: `pnpm bench:generate --capability inpaint` cuts a 4-bar gap out of each of the 5 fixtures
  (and `transition` across a fixture's mid-song bar line); report "≥ 2 of 3 pass on ≥ 4 of 5" for
  inpaint (the 12 acceptance says 4 of 5 fixtures). Run on ACE-Step, record.

### B. Engine + model: generated transitions, fill placement (Rust)

- Engine: `Transition { kind: "generated", asset, lengthTicks }` plays the asset as an overlay
  centred on the boundary with equal-power fades in and out, instead of the crossfade placeholder
  (02 §3.5, 03 §5.5); the two sections keep their own boundary crossfade underneath at the same
  length. Offline/context renders include it. WASM-vs-native conformance.
- Model: the smallest op/schema change for (a) a fill: a generated source placed in a **gap range**
  inside existing section(s) (today `Source.placement` names one section), its clip on the
  `generated` track, and the later stem mapping into that range only; (b) a transition: setting a
  generated transition with its asset, with provenance (09 §8). Pure placement helpers in
  `bragi_model` exposed through model-wasm, like E1's `extend_placement`. Undo exact; validate.

### C. API + planner: generation steps and execute (TypeScript)

- `jobs/capabilities.ts`: validators + credits for `generate.inpaint` (2 per 30 s) and
  `generate.transition` (1 flat), ownership checks on `before`/`after`.
- Planner: the capability catalogue lists `generate.extend|inpaint|transition` with their params;
  `generate` / `apply_generated` / `smooth` steps are validated (params against the capability
  schema, `ContextSpec` inside the song, placement region, policy on prompts, credits estimate)
  instead of rejected; system prompt v4 keeps "cheapest sufficient lever first" and allows at most
  one generative variant; bench cases with generation expectations (a few DeepSeek runs).
- Execute (06 §5): the client renders and uploads each `generate` step's context, then
  `POST /v1/plans/{id}/execute` with the step → context-asset map; the API enqueues one job per
  generate step (variants in parallel) and returns the job ids. Decide how plans are kept between
  propose and execute (stored row vs the client sending the plan back, re-validated) and record it.

### D. Client: Fill gap, Smooth with AI, generative plan variants (TypeScript/React)

- A generalised generation flow (from E2's extend flow) with per-capability adapters.
- **Fill gap** chip after an erase (07 §4.8) → inpaint → candidates in the Audition bar → accept →
  analysis of the generated asset → stems mapped into the gap.
- **✨ Smooth with AI** chip on a moved boundary (07 §4.1) → transition → candidates → accept sets the
  generated transition.
- Planner: a variant with `generate` steps executes into progressive candidates (ops applied
  immediately, `apply_generated` when its partial arrives, `smooth` compiled to ops), next to the
  ops-only variants in one Audition group.
- Live e2e on the fake backend: `fill-flow`, `transition-flow`, `planner-generate-flow` (faked
  planner response with a generative variant = M2 demo 4), all three browsers.

### E. Verification (Opus)

Reviews, `pnpm verify`, all live flows in three browsers, the ACE fill bench, one live DeepSeek
planner run of demo 4, outcome.

## Deviations

- **B — the gap is a range on the existing placement, not a placement union.** `SourcePlacement`
  gained two optional fields instead of becoming a `oneOf`: `range` (a `TickRange` **relative to the
  anchored section's start**, allowed to run into the following sections) and `srcStartSamples` (the
  candidate sample that plays at the gap start, i.e. the left overlap the gate crossfaded is
  skipped). An absent `range` keeps the extend meaning ("the whole section"), so every existing
  document and the E1 flow are unchanged, and the generated type only grows two `Option`s. Validation
  requires them together and bounds the range to the song.
- **B — a fill across a section boundary is supported with one clip per section.** Clips are
  windowed by their section (02 §3.5), so `fill_placement` splits the gap at every boundary it
  crosses and gives each slice its own clip on the `generated` track, all reading one continuous
  source window. The first slice fades in and the last fades out over the gate's crossfade length.
  The later stem mapping uses the same slices.
- **B — the stem mapping is driven by the placement span.** The reconciler maps the recorded
  `separation` effect over the placement's span (the whole section or the ranged gap) with
  `ClipPlan`'s new `base_samples` offset, so a fill's stems land on exactly the gap's samples. A
  placement with no lane audio at all (a generated transition) maps nothing and creates no tracks.
  `track.origin` is not set on a stem track created this way, and its derived ID names the role only
  (`trk_ derive:placed:stem:<role>`): several placed sources can map onto that one track, so naming
  a source or deriving from it would make the document depend on whose analysis arrived first. With
  both changes the cross-source order is deterministic too (test: two fills in one section, both
  mapping orders give canonical-equal documents).
- **B — the transition source has no lane clip and no `range`.** Its placement names the section it
  bridges, which is what keeps `analysis.apply` from building a baseline block for it (02 §6.4);
  because it has no clip, the reconciler leaves the document alone. The section's
  `transitionIn.asset` is the candidate's **working** asset (the 48 kHz copy the engine reads).
- **B — the generated transition keeps the crossfade underneath and is additive on top of it.**
  The task says "on top of the normal crossfade"; the overlay therefore does not duck the boundary
  crossfade. Measured on the WP-2.8 fixture (uncorrelated 55 Hz bed + 220 Hz bridge, 1 beat,
  **M2 Pro / MPS, not the reference GPU** — plain sample arithmetic): the overlay window measures
  −12.6 dBFS with the overlay, −15.1 dBFS with the placeholder, i.e. **+2.4 dB RMS** for the middle
  half; the safety limiter catches peaks. Ducking the crossfade by `√(1 − g²)` would keep the power
  constant and is recorded in 03 §5.5 as the follow-up if the owner hears a swell. A stem export
  leaves the overlay out (it belongs to the master bus), while mixdown and `context` renders
  include it.
- **B — the overlay is fitted to the transition length.** The asset's whole window is mapped
  linearly onto `[B − L/2, B + L/2]`, with `L` = `lengthTicks` at *B*'s tempo clamped to half of
  either section (the same clamp as the crossfade). A generated piece conformed to the grid (09 §6)
  is within a fraction of a percent of `L`, so the fit is a sub-percent ratio; it guarantees the
  quarter fades land on the asset's own edges. A missing asset, or one not in the `AssetIndex`,
  plans no overlay and keeps today's crossfade placeholder with a plan warning.
- **B — `ExtendCandidate` is now `GeneratedCandidate`.** Extend, fill and transition take the same
  candidate audio; the Rust type is shared rather than duplicated. It is a Rust-only rename — the
  request JSON is unchanged.

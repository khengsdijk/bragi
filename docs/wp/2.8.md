# WP-2.8 — Inpaint, erase & fill, AI transitions, planner generation steps

- **Spec refs:** [09 §4.3–4.4, §6](../../specs/09-generation.md), [06 §3, §5](../../specs/06-ai-planner.md), [07 §4.1, §4.8, §6](../../specs/07-song-canvas.md), [02 §3.5, §4](../../specs/02-song-model.md), [03 §5.5](../../specs/03-audio-engine.md)
- **Depends on:** 2.6, 2.7 (Done)
- **Size:** M (in practice L: three capabilities' worth of UI)
- **Branch:** `wp/2.8-generation`

## Goal

Generation inside the song, not only at its edges: **Fill gap** after an erase (`generate.inpaint`),
**Smooth with AI** on a section boundary after a move (`generate.transition`), and planner plans
whose variants include `generate` steps, so a lasso + prompt can come back with a generative idea
next to the ops-only ones.

**Acceptance (12):** M2 demo 4 (lasso + prompt with a generative variant) end to end. Fill-gap
candidates pass the gates on 4 of 5 fixtures.

## Contracts (first commit, Opus)

`packages/schemas/jobs/capabilities/`: `generate.inpaint.schema.json` (`GenerateInpaintParams`:
`context`, `gapBars`, `overlapBars` (default 1), `prompt?`, `seed?`, `tier`, `n`; input assets
`before` and `after`), `generate.transition.schema.json` (`GenerateTransitionParams`: `context`,
`lengthBars` 1–4, `prompt?`, `seed?`, `tier`, `n`; input assets `before` (end of A, ≤ 8 bars) and
`after` (start of B, ≤ 8 bars)), and one result blob for both, `generate.fill-result.schema.json`
(`GenerateFillResult`: like the extend result, spliced at both ends: `startOffsetSamples` relative to
the gap start, `endOffsetSamples` relative to the gap end, `seamScore` + `endSeamScore`). Job outputs
stream exactly like extend: `assets.candidate_<i>` + `blobs.candidate_<i>` per `partial`.

## Approach

### A. Worker: inpaint, transition, two-sided gates, fill bench (Python)

- `capabilities/inpaint.py` and `capabilities/transition.py` on the A2 machinery (routing, partial
  streaming, regeneration rounds, metrics). ACE-Step: native repaint of the middle span of
  `before + silence(gap) + after` (09 §4.3: native infill); transition = the same with
  `lengthBars` centred on the boundary. Fake backend: a deterministic fill from the contexts (loop
  of `before`'s last bars crossfaded into `after`), so the e2e passes the gates on any song.
- Two-sided gates: run 09 §6 at the start seam against `before` and, mirrored, at the end seam
  against `after` (B already mirrors for backward); loudness match uses `after` for the tail
  (WP-2.7 fix). Result blob per the contract.
- Register both in the generate worker, `capabilities.yaml`, `config/generation_routes.yaml`.
- Bench: `pnpm bench:generate --capability inpaint` cuts a 4-bar gap out of each of the 5 fixtures
  (and `transition` across a fixture's mid-song bar line); report "≥ 2 of 3 pass on ≥ 4 of 5" for
  inpaint (the 12 acceptance says 4 of 5 fixtures). Run on ACE-Step, record.

### B. Engine + model: generated transitions, fill placement (Rust)

- Engine: `Transition { kind: "generated", asset, lengthTicks }` plays the asset as an overlay
  centred on the boundary with equal-power fades in and out, instead of the crossfade placeholder
  (02 §3.5, 03 §5.5); the two sections keep their own boundary crossfade underneath at the same
  length. Offline/context renders include it. WASM-vs-native conformance.
- Model: the smallest op/schema change for (a) a fill: a generated source placed in a **gap range**
  inside existing section(s) (today `Source.placement` names one section), its clip on the
  `generated` track, and the later stem mapping into that range only; (b) a transition: setting a
  generated transition with its asset, with provenance (09 §8). Pure placement helpers in
  `bragi_model` exposed through model-wasm, like E1's `extend_placement`. Undo exact; validate.

### C. API + planner: generation steps and execute (TypeScript)

- `jobs/capabilities.ts`: validators + credits for `generate.inpaint` (2 per 30 s) and
  `generate.transition` (1 flat), ownership checks on `before`/`after`.
- Planner: the capability catalogue lists `generate.extend|inpaint|transition` with their params;
  `generate` / `apply_generated` / `smooth` steps are validated (params against the capability
  schema, `ContextSpec` inside the song, placement region, policy on prompts, credits estimate)
  instead of rejected; system prompt v4 keeps "cheapest sufficient lever first" and allows at most
  one generative variant; bench cases with generation expectations (a few DeepSeek runs).
- Execute (06 §5): the client renders and uploads each `generate` step's context, then
  `POST /v1/plans/{id}/execute` with the step → context-asset map; the API enqueues one job per
  generate step (variants in parallel) and returns the job ids. Decide how plans are kept between
  propose and execute (stored row vs the client sending the plan back, re-validated) and record it.

### D. Client: Fill gap, Smooth with AI, generative plan variants (TypeScript/React)

- A generalised generation flow (from E2's extend flow) with per-capability adapters.
- **Fill gap** chip after an erase (07 §4.8) → inpaint → candidates in the Audition bar → accept →
  analysis of the generated asset → stems mapped into the gap.
- **✨ Smooth with AI** chip on a moved boundary (07 §4.1) → transition → candidates → accept sets the
  generated transition.
- Planner: a variant with `generate` steps executes into progressive candidates (ops applied
  immediately, `apply_generated` when its partial arrives, `smooth` compiled to ops), next to the
  ops-only variants in one Audition group.
- Live e2e on the fake backend: `fill-flow`, `transition-flow`, `planner-generate-flow` (faked
  planner response with a generative variant = M2 demo 4), all three browsers.

### E. Verification (Opus)

Reviews, `pnpm verify`, all live flows in three browsers, the ACE fill bench, one live DeepSeek
planner run of demo 4, outcome.

## A — Worker: `generate.inpaint`, `generate.transition`, two-sided gates, fill bench

**Backend contract.** `GenRequest` is unchanged. Two-sided requests are a sibling
`FillRequest` in `backend.py`: both contexts, `gap_bars`, `lead_bars` (how many of the
generated middle's bars lie before the join; 0 for an inpaint, `lengthBars / 2` for a
transition) and `overlap_bars`. The candidate is `overlap + (gap + 2·lead) + overlap`
bars; the outer overlaps re-create the audio around the span, so the two splice
searches have real context. `GenerationBackend.generate_fill()` is the second call;
extend keeps `generate()` and both adapters keep their old path.

**ACE-Step mapping (the fields used).** A fill is A2's explicit-span
`RepaintRequest`: `task_type="repaint"`, `src_audio_path` = `before + silence(gap) +
after` (no silence for a transition), `repainting_start` = `before_end - lead -
overlap`, `repainting_end` = `before_end + gap + lead + overlap`,
`chunk_mask_mode="explicit"`, `repaint_mode="balanced"`, `repaint_strength=0.5`,
`audio_format="wav32"`, plus bpm/key/time signature/seed. ACE-Step's waveform splice
(`acestep/core/generation/handler/repaint_waveform_splice.py`) replaces only the span
with a 10 ms ramp at each edge and preserves the rest sample for sample, so the
candidate is `returned[span_start:span_end]`; the A2 gain undo measures ACE's −1 dBFS
normalisation on the preserved before part. The fake backend loops the left context's
last bars across the span and equal-power crossfades into the right context's head
over the gate's crossfade length, so its head is an exact re-creation and its tail is
exact from one crossfade on.

**Capabilities and shared code.** `capabilities/fill.py` loads
`inputs.assets.before`/`after`, builds the request and calls
`capabilities/streaming.py`, which is extend's round/seed/partial/metrics loop
factored out (extend, inpaint and transition all use it). `inpaint.py` sets
`gapBars`/`overlapBars`; `transition.py` sets `gap_bars=0` and
`lead_bars=lengthBars/2`. Both expose `run_gates` as the test seam and return a
`GenerateFillResult` per candidate. `capabilities/blobs.py` is the shared key/match_eq/
step/upload half of the two result schemas.

**Two-sided gates.** `postprocess.postprocess_fill()` runs steps 1–4 once against the
left context (the candidate's head re-creates its tail), step 5 at the start seam with
extend's forward search and at the end seam with the roles swapped — the candidate's
tail is the context and `after` continues it, the backward-extend mirror in playback
order — step 6 with `after=` (the WP-2.7 loudness fix), and step 7 at both seams. The
`FillGateResult` carries `startOffsetSamples`/`endOffsetSamples` and both scores; the
blob's seven steps keep their names, with the end seam's numbers in the `splice` and
`final_seam` metrics prefixed `end_`.

## Deviations

- **A — the bench asks for `overlapBars = 2`, not the schema's default 1.** With one
  bar the inpaint candidate is six bars (1 overlap + 4 gap + 1 overlap). On the house
  and waltz fixtures the key detector reads the dominant (D major) on every such
  candidate — including the fake backend's loop of the in-key context — so the key
  gate rejected all nine ACE attempts although the model was in key. Two bars give an
  eight-bar candidate and the reading stabilises: the ACE house passed 3/3, the waltz
  2/2 after regeneration, and the fake passed 3/3 on both. The capability still honours
  the param; the client should send 2, or the schema default should change to 2 (open
  issue, `packages/` is another task's).
- **A — the transition uses two bars of overlap.** Its schema has no overlap field, so
  the capability chooses. One bar made a four-bar candidate whose key/beat reading was
  unstable (the fake hiphop failed the start seam at 1.18); `TRANSITION_OVERLAP_BARS =
  2` gives a six-bar candidate and the fake hiphop then passed.
- **A — the processed clip's fades are applied after the seam scores.** The clip
  keeps a short head fade for auditioning, but both scores use the unfaded span: the
  same render the splice search minimises. Applying a fade-out before the end score
  crossfaded the already-faded tail again and inflated the score by about 0.8 (a seam
  whose search score was 0.046 read 0.85); the unfaded render scores 0.045.
- **A — the fill splice search falls back to the requested grid.** A one-bar overlap
  leaves 0.5 s of search room after the 0.5 s seam window and the one-beat fade; when
  the tracker locks to half time, no tracked beat lands there and the search returns
  "no splice position". `splice_search(..., grid_fallback=True)` adds the requested
  tempo's grid anchored on the first tracked beat. Only the fill calls set it, so
  extend's search and the WP-2.7 numbers are unchanged.
- **A — the two-sided result keeps the seven step names.** The contract's example has
  the extend seven; the end seam's numbers live in the `splice` and `final_seam`
  metrics prefixed `end_` (with `seamScore` and `endSeamScore` at the top level), so
  the client reads one step list for both capabilities.
- **A — `BenchContext` takes an asset map.** A fill has two inputs; extend's
  single-payload constructor path is unchanged. The bench's fill preview is the app's
  join (context up to the clip's start offset, the clip, the context from its end
  offset), not the gate's scoring render.
- **A — kept-audio names.** Extend keeps `splices/A.wav` and `listening-order.json`
  (the 2.7 sheet points at them); fills prefix with the capability
  (`splices/inpaint-A.wav`, `listening-order-inpaint.json`) so both runs share one
  `bench-results/` without overwriting each other.
- **A — the fake backend is the smoke test, not the check.** With `n = 3` and two
  bars of overlap it reaches two passing candidates on 5 of 5 inpaint songs and 3 of 5
  transition songs (house and waltz read D major for every seed). The transition's
  fake loops the left context into the right one, so its key follows whichever
  section the loop ends in — a limitation of the fake, not the capability. The ACE
  runs are the acceptance.

## Goal

An `analysis.harmony` capability that estimates the **key** and the **chords** of a song from the
harmonic stems and derives a **key-invariant** `progressionFingerprint`, plus the ADR that records
the chord/key-detection decision.

**Acceptance (from [12](../../specs/12-roadmap.md)):**
1. Chord accuracy (majmin, duration-weighted) ≥ 0.70 **on the eval set**.
2. Key accuracy ≥ 0.80 **on the eval set**.
3. `progressionFingerprint` is key-invariant: a transposed fixture gives the same fingerprint.

Criteria 1 and 2 need the eval set, which **does not exist** (Q32). This WP ships the machinery and
marks them **PENDING**; criterion 3 is proven by a test.

## Environment constraints (stated up front)

- macOS 15 / arm64 (Apple M2 Pro). **No CUDA GPU.** Everything runs on CPU (13 §3).
- **No eval set** (`fixtures/eval/manifest.json` lists no tracks; the audio lives in a private bucket
  that does not exist yet). No chord/key accuracy number is claimed. The pipeline is proven on
  **synthetic** signals with known chords and keys, reported as synthetic, never as the gate.
- **No model weights are introduced.** The v1 recogniser is weight-free (below), so nothing is added
  to `MODELS.md` as a pinned model and nothing is committed or downloaded.
- No copyrighted audio; every fixture is generated by the test (13 §1.7).

## Approach

### Contracts

`packages/schemas/analysis/harmony.schema.json` (`HarmonyResult`: `key`, `keyChanges?`, `chords[]`,
`sections[] {index, romanNumerals, progressionFingerprint}`) already exists (WP-0.2). **One field is
missing:** 04 §10 (and the `rhythm` and `structure` schemas) publishes a top-level `lowConfidence`
flag for a degraded result, and `HarmonyResult` has none. This WP adds `lowConfidence` (optional
boolean, like `rhythm`) to the schema, regenerates (`pnpm schemas:gen`), and uses it for
silent/short/ambiguous input. That is the only contract change.

Because the orchestrator currently passes only `assets.source` to every stage (Q33), the
capability accepts harmonic stems **when they are present** (`assets.stem_bass`, `stem_other`,
`stem_piano`, `stem_guitar`, as 04 §5.3 asks) and degrades to `assets.mix`, then `assets.source`,
logging which it used.

### Decision: weight-free beat-synchronous chroma + template decoding (ADR-0008)

04 §5.3 names "a neural chord recogniser (candidate: a transformer chord model, licence verified in
WP-1.5)" and cautions that many well-known key/chord tools are AGPL/GPL or non-commercial (madmom's
pretrained models are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — already rejected for beats in ADR-0007).

Given no GPU, no eval set and the licence caution, the v1 recogniser is **weight-free**:
beat-synchronous chroma matched against a small chord-template bank with a Viterbi decoder, and key
estimation by Krumhansl–Schmuckler profile correlation refined by the chord sequence. This mirrors
WP-1.2's `BeatTracker` protocol: a `ChordRecognizer` seam is left for a neural backend (and its
pinned weights) once a permissively licensed one is chosen and the eval set can compare them.
**[ADR-0008](../../specs/decisions/ADR-0008-chord-detection.md)** records this and lists the
candidates (BTC, ChordFormer, madmom) and why none is adopted yet.

No new dependency: chroma uses the existing STFT (`dsp/features.py`), the decoders are
`numpy`/`scipy`, and the synthetic proof uses `mir_eval.chord` (already a dependency).

### Module layout (`services/workers/analysis`)

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `capabilities/harmony.py` | `CAPABILITY = "analysis.harmony"`, `ParamsModel`, `run(job, ctx)`: download harmonic stems, decode+sum, optional beats/sections from `inputs.blobs`, build and upload the `HarmonyResult` |
| `harmony/chroma.py` | STFT → 12-D pitch-class chroma, L2-normalised; beat synchronisation |
| `harmony/chords.py` | The chord-template bank and the change-penalised Viterbi decoder → `ChordSegment`s; the `ChordRecognizer` protocol and `select()` (the neural seam) |
| `harmony/key.py` | Krumhansl–Schmuckler key estimation (refined by the chords), key-change detection, roman numerals and the `progressionFingerprint` |
| `harmony/pipeline.py` | Pure orchestration: chroma → key → chords → numerals → sections → `HarmonyResult` |

`harmony/*` imports no I/O or ffmpeg, so the numeric core is tested on arrays directly.

### Algorithm

1. **Chroma.** Resample to 11 025 Hz (working assets are 48 kHz), STFT (n_fft 4096, hop 1024), fold
   each bin in 65–2100 Hz into its nearest pitch class, L2-normalise each frame, and keep the raw
   band energy per frame for silence detection.
2. **Beat synchronisation.** When beats are available (an optional `rhythm` blob), one averaged
   chroma vector per beat interval; otherwise the frame grid is used directly. Chord segments then
   land on beat boundaries when rhythm has run.
3. **Key.** Pearson-correlate the energy-weighted mean chroma with the 24 rotated Krumhansl
   profiles, then re-estimate from a 50/50 mix of the chroma and the duration-weighted chord
   templates ("refined by the chord sequence"). `confidence` is a scaled margin over the runner-up;
   silence → confidence 0. Key changes are detected on ~8 s windows with a two-window run
   requirement.
4. **Chords.** 12 roots × a template bank (maj, min, dim, aug, sus2/4, maj7, min7, dom7, min7b5,
   dim7, maj6, min6, minMaj7) plus a "no chord" state; a Viterbi pass with a change penalty yields
   stable segments. Each segment carries the mean cosine match as its confidence; sub-`minChordSec`
   segments are merged away. Digital-silence frames decode to "no chord" and are dropped.
5. **Roman numerals.** Case from the quality (lowercase for min/dim/half-dim, uppercase otherwise),
   diatonic degree with a `b`/`#` accidental for chromatic roots, a quality suffix (`7`, `°`, `+`,
   `maj7`, …). Because the numerals are relative to the tonic, a transposed song gives the same ones.
6. **Fingerprint.** The roman-numeral sequence with immediate repeats collapsed, joined with `-`
   (e.g. `i-VI-III-VII`). Per section when a `structure` blob (or explicit sections) is available,
   otherwise one whole-song progression at `index: 0`.
7. **Degrade, never fail.** Silence, a clip too short for a chroma frame, or a key that cannot be
   told apart publishes `chords: []` and/or a low-confidence key with `lowConfidence: true`; the
   stage never raises for content it cannot analyse (04 §1.3, §10).

### Test plan

| Test | Needs weights/ffmpeg | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| chroma mapping | no | a C-major triad puts its energy on C/E/G; empty input is zero |
| key estimation | no | a synthetic progression is read as the right tonic/mode; white noise/silence is not |
| chord decoding | no | synthetic triads/sevenths give the right chords and a high majmin accuracy (`mir_eval.chord`) |
| roman numerals | no | degree, case, accidentals (`bVII`), and the example `i-VI-III-VII` |
| **transposition invariance** | no | the same progression in C and in D gives the **same `progressionFingerprint`** (acceptance 3) |
| sections | no | structure sections align progressions; a whole-song fallback uses `index 0` |
| capability `run` | **ffmpeg** | fake ctx + stems: schema-valid `HarmonyResult`, harmonic stems summed, progress, cancel |
| graceful degradation | ffmpeg | silent, sub-frame-short and noise inputs give `lowConfidence: true`, never a failure |

The real eval gates (chord/key accuracy on the eval set) are **PENDING**; the synthetic numbers are
labelled synthetic.

## Spec gaps found

1. **`HarmonyResult` had no `lowConfidence`** while 04 §10 and the sibling schemas do. Added
   (schema-first) in this WP.
2. **"`progressionFingerprint` per section" is undefined before `structure` exists.** 04 §5.3 derives
   it per section, but `harmony` depends only on `separation` in the DAG, and `structure` may never
   run (the `quick` profile). This WP emits one whole-song progression at `index: 0` when no sections
   are supplied and aligns to the structure sections when they are. Recorded as a question.
3. **"Normalised roman-numeral n-gram sequence"** is implemented as the collapsed (1-gram) numeral
   sequence that the example shows; true n-grams for the constellations similarity (08 §5) are left
   open. Recorded as a question.
4. **Beat-synchronous chroma needs beats**, which come from `rhythm`, a sibling of `harmony` (not a
   dependency). This WP uses a `rhythm` blob if the orchestrator passes one and a frame grid
   otherwise. Recorded as a question.
5. **Slash chords (`bass`)** and a neural recogniser are not in v1; root-position templates only.
   Recorded as a question.
6. The eval harness (WP-1.2) scores `rhythm` only; chord/key scoring needs a decision before the
   gates can run. Recorded as a question.

### Proofs (measured, macOS 15 / arm64, CPU)

- Synthetic chords: a I-IV-V-I loop decodes to exactly `C-F-G-C`; seventh chords to
  `Cmaj7-Am7-Dm7-G7`; majmin accuracy 0.990 duration-weighted.
- Key: I-IV-V-I in C reads C major, i-iv-V-i in A minor reads A minor, D major reads D major.
- Key-invariance: 5 transpositions of one progression → one fingerprint.
- Degradation: digital silence → `chords: []`, `sections` omitted, `lowConfidence: true`; a 0.3 s
  clip and white noise → `lowConfidence: true`; none raises.
- Speed: 6.6 min of signal — chroma 0.10 s, decode + key 0.04 s (frame-level, single core). The
  capability adds one `ffmpeg` decode per input; a real 4-min song stays well inside the
  `standard` profile budget.
- Tests: **29 harmony tests pass**; the whole worker suite is **241 passed, 2 skipped** (the
  weights-gated Beat This! and separation tests). `ruff check`, `ruff format --check` and
  `mypy --strict` are clean.

### Deviations

- `HarmonyResult` gained `lowConfidence` (04 §10 applies to every stage; `rhythm`/`structure` already
  had it). Added schema-first and regenerated.
- When `structure` has not run, the progressions are published as one whole-song entry at `index: 0`
  (Q61) instead of leaving `sections` empty, so the fingerprint the acceptance needs always exists.
- Beat-synchronous chroma is used only when a `rhythm` blob is passed (Q63); otherwise the frame
  grid is decoded.

### Deferred / not done

- The eval gates (chord ≥ 0.70, key ≥ 0.80) and any accuracy claim on real music (Q32, Q65).
- A neural chord recogniser and slash chords (`C/E`) (Q64); the key-change detector is a heuristic
  that the eval set should calibrate.
- Not exercised through NATS and the orchestrator DAG; the orchestrator still passes `assets.source`
  rather than the stems (Q33). Not run off macOS arm64 (Q11).

# WP-2.3 — Manual checks

Listening checks for the FX catalogue and the vibe sliders. Run on the owner's speakers or
headphones with `pnpm dev:backend` + `pnpm dev:web`, on a real song (upload, wait for the stems).

1. **Vibe sliders while playing.** Press Play, select a stem (click its ribbon), and move each of
   the six sliders slowly from one end to the other, then fast. Listen for: no clicks, pops or
   zipper noise while the slider moves; the change is audible and goes the way the label says
   (Darker ↔ Brighter, Dry ↔ Spacious, Soft ↔ Punchy, Clean ↔ Warm, Modern ↔ Vintage,
   Close ↔ Distant).
2. **Undo while playing.** After a Space change, press Cmd+Z: the reverb tail fades naturally
   (no cut-off click), and the slider returns to "Unchanged".
3. **Expert drawer.** Open Expert, change a filter cutoff and a compressor threshold with the
   keyboard and the mouse: smooth, no steps. Bypass an effect and re-enable it: no click.
4. **Alignment.** Put a saturation on the drums only and solo-unsolo the drums against the other
   stems: no comb-filter "phasiness" from the 30-frame oversampling delay (delay compensation).
5. **Master limiter.** Add a limiter on the master and push a loud passage: no audible pumping at
   the default release; the exported mixdown starts exactly on bar 1 (latency trimmed).

Record results (pass/fail, notes, machine and output device) under each item.

# WP-2.5 — Audition engine & Audition bar

- **Spec refs:** [03 §5.4](../../specs/03-audio-engine.md), [07 §6, §7, §10 check 4](../../specs/07-song-canvas.md), [02 §7](../../specs/02-song-model.md)
- **Depends on:** 1.1 (Done), 2.1 (Done)
- **Size:** M
- **Branch:** `wp/2.5-audition`

## Goal

Hear alternatives instantly. The engine keeps up to four plans warm (HEAD + three candidates) and
switches between them at the same playhead with a 30 ms crossfade. The Audition bar shows a
candidate group, switches with `0–3`, and accepts, discards or asks for more like one. The History
panel's **Compare (A/B)** (deferred from WP-2.1) uses the same machinery.

**Acceptance (12):** 07 §10 check 4 (switching between 4 warm plans has no gap or click: no sample
discontinuity step > −40 dBFS at the switch, verified by render capture). Candidate state badges
update from job events. Accept/discard/more-like-this follow 02 §7.

## What exists

- The transport has an `auditioning` overlay flag only; there are no warm plans.
- `EngineHost.audition(target | null)` exists in the platform types and only sends the flag.
- History already supports candidate commits: `addCandidates(batches, group)`, `accept(id)`,
  `discardGroup(group)` (WP-2.1), and the History panel multi-selects for Compare.
- Nothing produces candidates yet: AI requests arrive with WP-2.6 (planner) and WP-2.7
  (generation). This WP builds the audition machinery and drives it with Compare and with
  candidate groups created in tests.

## Approach

### A. Engine (`bragi-engine`, both hosts)

- `Renderer` holds up to `MAX_WARM_PLANS = 4` prepared plans (slot 0 = HEAD) with their own
  segment runtimes, fx boxes and delay lines, prepared off the audio thread like `set_prepared`.
  Only the active plan and, during a switch, the outgoing one render; the others stay prepared but
  idle (their stretchers re-prime on activation — measure that this stays inside the crossfade or
  pre-roll a few blocks).
- `audition(slot | None)`: a 30 ms equal-power crossfade from the current plan to the target at the
  same playhead. A switch during a running crossfade restarts it from the current mix.
- API: `warm(slot, plan)`, `release(slot)`, `audition(slot | None)`; the wasm and native hosts expose
  them; the browser host sends assets for a warm plan's new hashes once (the cold-load fix's
  `loaded` set).
- Tests: render capture switching between four plans of the same fixture with different gains/fx
  at random points: no step > −40 dBFS beyond the signal's own step at the switch (the WP-2.2
  boundary metric); crossfade length 30 ms ± 1 block; no allocation in `process` while switching;
  conformance (WASM vs native) on a switch fixture.

### B. Platform + app state

- `EngineHost.warm(slot, doc)`, `release(slot)`, `audition(slot | null)` replace the stub (contract
  update in `packages/platform` and 03 §5.4).
- `features/audition/state/audition.ts`: a store for the active candidate group — candidates (commit
  ids, labels, status `queued | generating n% | preview | HQ`), the selected slot, the loop region
  (affected region + 2 bars each side, 07 §6). Status comes from a `CandidateSource` that maps job
  events (`JobEvent` schema: progress, partial, done) to statuses; WP-2.6/2.7 plug real jobs in, this
  WP tests it with fake events.
- Accept → `history.accept(id)` (the sibling candidates become discarded, HEAD moves) and the engine
  keeps playing from the accepted plan without a gap. Discard → `history.discardGroup(group)` and
  audition back to HEAD. More like this → emits a `moreLike(candidateId)` request (the generator
  arrives with 2.6/2.7; until then the button is hidden when no source can serve it). Only one
  group at a time (07 §6).
- Compare (A/B) from the History panel: two selected versions become a temporary audition group
  (no new commits), `A`/`B` switch, Esc closes.

### C. Audition bar UI

The bar from 07 §6: prompt label, candidate chips with status badges, `0–3` keys, More like,
Discard, Accept; auto-loop of the region + context and auto-play on open; canvas shows the selected
candidate's ribbons (render the candidate document) with changed areas outlined; accessible
(toolbar role, live-region announcements "3 version ideas ready, playing idea 1", 11 §6.3).

### D. Verification (Opus)

Vitest (store, status mapping, accept/discard), engine tests above, `pnpm verify`, and live:
open Compare on two versions of a real song while playing, switch A/B repeatedly (no gap; listen),
accept; axe on the open bar in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.

## Deviations

- **API:** `audition(slot)` takes `0 | 1 | 2 | 3` (0 = HEAD) instead of `slot | null`, so the
  web host can send it as one command word; `warm` resolves once the slot is ready. 03 §5.4
  describes the slots, the 30 ms crossfade and what happens to a replaced or released plan.
- **Refused warms:** a slot that is audible or still fading out cannot be replaced (the engine
  returns the plan instead of dropping it on the audio thread). The audition store retries a
  refused warm once after the fade (200 ms) and cancels a closed group's pending release of a
  slot the next group warms. Found by the live spec: a group opened right after a comparison
  closed lost a slot.
- **Accept:** the new HEAD reaches the engine through the normal commit path; installing it
  while a candidate is audible crossfades to it (no `audition(0)` jump back to the old HEAD).
  Slots are released 200 ms later, after that crossfade.
- **Compare** is a temporary group with no Accept: `A`/`B` (and `0` for HEAD) switch, Escape
  or Close returns to HEAD. Escape does nothing on an AI candidate group: leaving one needs an
  explicit Accept or Discard (07 §6: one group at a time).
- **Canvas:** the canvas and the accessible grid draw the selected candidate's document. The
  "changed areas outlined" part of 07 §6 is **not built**: it needs a per-region diff of two
  documents in the renderer, which belongs with the first real candidates (WP-2.6/2.7).
- **More like this** is hidden until a `CandidateSource` offers `moreLike` (WP-2.6/2.7). No
  source exists yet; the store maps `JobEvent`s onto the badges and is tested with fake events.
- **History panel:** rows that are not committed are now faded with the muted text colour
  instead of 55 % opacity. The live audition spec's axe scan found that the old opacity made
  the "Candidate" label fail contrast (the first live run with candidates in the tree).

## Outcome

Landed 2026-10-20. Everything was run on an **Apple M2 Pro (macOS)**, not the reference machine.

**Acceptance (12):**

- *07 §10 check 4:* four warm plans of one fixture (different gains, an fx, a muted track)
  switched at random playheads while playing: the maximum sample step within ±30 ms of every
  switch stays within +0.01 of the signal's own maximum step (−30.6 vs −33.3 dBFS); a 0 ms
  switch measures −12.1 dBFS and fails the same check. A switch inside a running crossfade is
  click-free, the crossfade lasts 30 ms ± one block, the playhead is continuous, a switch into a
  stretched plan does not dip, and `no_alloc` stays at 0 while switching. Conformance fixture
  `engine-conformance-f-audition` nulls WASM vs native at −184.9 dBFS.
- *Candidate state badges update from job events:* `statusFromJobEvent` maps
  started/progress/partial/completed/failed onto queued → generating n% → preview → HQ; a
  candidate is warmed when its preview lands and warmed again for HQ (tests with fake events).
- *Accept/discard/more-like-this follow 02 §7:* Accept commits the selected candidate through
  `history.accept` (HEAD moves, siblings discarded), Discard marks the group discarded and
  returns to HEAD, More like this is forwarded to the source.

**Timing:** four warm plans × 6 tracks × 3 fx: 0.51 % of real time (release).

**Live e2e** (`apps/web/e2e/audition-flow.spec.ts`, real stack, while playing): mute a stem →
History → pick both versions → Compare → B/A five times (0 underruns, playback continues) →
axe → Escape returns to HEAD with nothing committed → three candidates through the test hooks →
2/3/0/2 → Accept → HEAD is candidate 2, the other two are discarded, playback continues. Green
in **Chromium, Firefox and WebKit**. `pnpm test:e2e:flow` now has 7 specs (2.2 min).

**Not verified by ear:** that the switches *sound* gapless on a real song in a browser (the
render-capture test covers the engine).

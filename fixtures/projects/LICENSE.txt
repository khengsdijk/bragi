is no audio, lyrics, artwork or third-party dataset here: the documents use
invented titles, labels, IDs and hashes. They may be copied, modified and
redistributed under the same terms as the rest of the repository
(`"license": "UNLICENSED"` in the root package.json), and they are NOT derived
from any copyrighted recording. See specs/10-fusion.md §7 and
specs/13-agent-guide.md §1.

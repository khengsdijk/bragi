Licence register for fixtures/audio
===================================

committed. Permitted origins: self-made, CC0 / public domain, or generated by a
script in this repository. No copyrighted audio (specs/13-agent-guide.md §1).

Format:

  <filename>
    Origin:  self-made | CC0 | generated by <script>
    Licence: <licence and URL, or "CC0 1.0">
    Notes:   <sample rate, channels, what it is for>

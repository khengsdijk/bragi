Source register for fixtures/audio
==================================

Every audio file in this directory MUST have an entry below before it is
committed. Permitted origins: self-made, or generated by a script in this
repository. No copyrighted audio (specs/13-agent-guide.md §1).

Format:

  <filename>
    Origin:  self-made | generated by <script>
    Notes:   <sample rate, channels, what it is for>

Entries
-------

drums_120.wav
  Origin:  generated by crates/bragi-dsp/stretch-lab/lab/src/bin/gen_fixtures.rs
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 4 s. Two-bar 120 BPM drum loop (kick/snare/hat) for the
           time-stretch "no flamming" check and the pitch-shift listening check.

tone_440.wav
  Origin:  generated by crates/bragi-dsp/stretch-lab/lab/src/bin/gen_fixtures.rs
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 2 s. 440 Hz sine, for pitch-shift listening.

golden/engine-conformance-a.wav
  Origin:  rendered by `cargo run -p bragi-engine-native --bin bragi-render`
           (scripts/conformance.mjs --update) from fixtures/audio/drums_120.wav
           and tone_440.wav
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 32-bit float, 2 s. Golden mixdown for the
           WASM-vs-native null test (specs/03-audio-engine.md §11).

golden/engine-conformance-b.wav
  Origin:  rendered by scripts/conformance.mjs --update from tone_440.wav
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 32-bit float, 1 s. Golden mixdown with fades, pan,
           track gain and a reversed clip.

golden/engine-conformance-c-stretch.wav
  Origin:  rendered by scripts/conformance.mjs --update from tone_440.wav
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 32-bit float, 1 s. Golden mixdown through the phase
           vocoder (informational: the cross-target null is reported, not gated;
           see Q31 in specs/15-open-questions.md).

golden/engine-conformance-d-fx.wav
  Origin:  rendered by scripts/conformance.mjs --update from tone_440.wav
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 32-bit float, 1 s. Golden mixdown through a track
           and a master `gain` (WP-2.3).

golden/engine-conformance-e-boundaries.wav
  Origin:  rendered by scripts/conformance.mjs --update from drums_120.wav and
           tone_440.wav
  Notes:   48000 Hz, 2 ch, 32-bit float, 2 s. Golden mixdown with four reordered
           sections, three 5 ms cut crossfades and one 1/4-beat crossfade
           (specs/03-audio-engine.md §5.5, WP-2.2).
